  # 🎯 State of the Dart

  > **The dart scorer for the pub, the basement and the club: X01, Cricket, Shanghai and training with live stats, heatmaps, 463 achievements, online multiplayer and a TV scoreboard — one installable web app for phone, tablet, laptop and TV.**

  Type the total, press Enter — the next player is up at once. The big card shows who is at the oche, the remaining score, the checkout route and the place in the leg; a 180 takes the screen, and the TV follows every visit live. Works offline, syncs through your account, no ads.

  ### ✨ What it does (in short)

  - 🎯 **X01 up to 1001** with single, double or triple in/out, sets and legs, bots in 10 levels
  - ⌨️ **Fast scoring:** type the total + Enter, single darts, quick scores or the board — undo across leg and set boundaries
  - 🔦 **Spotlight game screen:** a big card for the player at the oche with checkout route, place in the leg and a burning frame
  - 📐 **Every screen size:** input in thumb reach on phones, two panes on tablets, three with the board on laptops, no width cap on XXL monitors
  - 📺 **TV scoreboard** on a second screen, live with every visit
  - 🎉 **Feedback ladder:** sparks from 100, the 180 moment, a leg recap, personal bests live, haptics per level
  - 🎡 **Wheel of fortune** for the throw-off, with real physics
  - 📊 **Stats & heatmaps** bed-accurate, history, comparison, leaderboards
  - 🏆 **463 achievements** in five tiers with their own shapes
  - 🎮 **Cricket, Around the Clock, Shanghai, 6 training modes, tournaments, online multiplayer**
  - 🔊 **Caller with 609 voice files** · 🌍 German and English · ♿ WCAG 2.1 AA checked
  - 📴 **Offline-first PWA** with account sync, resume after a refresh, no ads

  ### 🧰 Tech stack

  React 19 · TypeScript 5 · Vite · Tailwind · Material 3 Expressive (own springs, shapes, icons) · Framer Motion · WebGL · Express · SQLite · Socket.IO · Workbox. **1188 unit tests + 46 E2E tests.** MIT-licensed.

## ✨ Features

### 👥 Multi-User & Authentication System
- **User Registration** - Email registration with verification
- **Secure Authentication** - JWT-based authentication with bcrypt
- **Google OAuth** - Quick sign-in with Google account
- **30-Day Trial Period** - Free trial for all new users
- **Stripe Integration** - Monthly or lifetime subscriptions
- **Personal Profiles** - Each user has isolated profiles with separate data
- **Profile Management** - Easy switching between profiles with visual avatars
- **Data Isolation** - Complete separation of stats, settings, and game history
- **Cloud Sync** - Automatic cloud data backup
- **👑 Admin System** - Complete user management for administrators

### 🎮 Game Modes
- **X01 Games** - Full support for 301/501/701/1001 with customizable settings
- **Check-in / check-out** - single, double or triple each (e.g. double out, triple out with finishes up to 180)
- **Cricket, Around the Clock, Shanghai, Online Multiplayer** - additional modes, all resumable
- **Best of Sets/Legs** - Tournament-style match formats
- **Multi-player** - Support for 2+ players with custom avatars and names
- **Continue Match** - Resume interrupted games automatically
- **Training Modes** - 6 training modes including Doubles/Triples practice, Around the Clock, and Bob's 27
- **Tournament System** - knockout and round robin, stored in the database

### 🖥️ Game screen "Arena" & TV scoreboard (v0.28–v0.31)
- **Spotlight** — the player at the oche gets a large hero card (huge remaining score, checkout route, place in the leg, darts in the leg, average, legs to win, last visits); everyone else is a compact row. When the turn passes, the next row grows into the hero card as a whole (M3 container transform) — text is never stretched.
- **Every screen size** (M3 window size classes) — phone: input docked at the bottom, stats/history/heatmap/board in a bottom sheet with tabs; tablet landscape: two panes; laptop and up: three panes with the board alongside; very large monitors without a narrow width cap.
- **Insights below the board** — match stats (all players side by side), visit history, throw chart and live heatmap as always-open cards in two columns.
- **Feedback ladder** — big Roboto Flex numerals that swell on every change; sparks from 100, a 180 takes the screen (circle → burst morph, confetti), a leg recap with darts/average/checkout, new personal bests live — never blocking, one haptic pattern per level.
- **"Who throws first?" wheel** — spins with real physics (friction + drag), a flapper clicks on every peg with sound and haptics, the winning wedge steps forward.
- **TV scoreboard** (`/scoreboard`) — the screen button in the game opens the score in its own window for a TV, projector or second monitor: the active player's remaining score at about a third of the screen height, the live visit, checkout route, every player with place and remaining score, moments for 180/100+/bust/leg and the winner. Updates with every visit at once (same device and browser, no server), full screen button, the pointer hides when idle. The window only reads and can never write a score back.

### 📊 Advanced Statistics & Charts
- **10+ Interactive Charts** - Beautiful visualizations with Recharts
  - Radar Chart: Performance profile (Average, Checkout %, 180s, Win Rate)
  - Pie Chart: Win/Loss statistics
  - Bar Charts: Score distribution, High Scores
  - Line Charts: Average and checkout progression
  - Round Chart: Match progression round by round (3 darts = 1 round)
  - Area Charts: Legs won/lost
  - Composed Charts: Monthly performance trends
- **Player Comparison** - Compare up to 4 players side-by-side with:
  - Radar Chart: 5-dimensional skill comparison
  - Stats Table: Detailed head-to-head comparison
  - Bar Chart: Visual metric comparison
- **Real-Time Stats** - Live scoring with instant calculations
- **Player Statistics** - Average, checkout %, high scores, 180s, 171+, 140+, 100+
- **Match History** - Complete tracking of all games played
- **Trend Analysis** - Improvement metrics and performance trends
- **Personal Bests** - Track highest checkouts, best averages, 9-darters
- **Multi-Format Export (NEW in v0.1.7)** - Export stats in 3 formats:
  - **CSV Export** - Text-based, compatible with Excel/Google Sheets
  - **Excel Export (.xlsx)** - Native Excel files with summary sheet
  - **PDF Export** - Professional reports with formatted tables
- **Export/Import** - JSON for full data backup
- **Automatic Sync** - Stats updated automatically after each match

### 🏆 Achievements & Gamification
- **20 Achievements** - Across 6 categories (First Steps, Scoring, Checkout, Training, Consistency, Special)
- **Tier System** - Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond tiers
- **Rarity Levels** - Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary achievements
- **Progress Tracking** - See progress for locked achievements
- **Notifications** - Animated popups when achievements are unlocked
- **Points System** - Earn points for achievements (up to 500 per achievement)
- **Hidden Achievements** - Special secret achievements to discover

### 👤 Player Profiles & Leaderboard
- **Detailed Player Profiles** - Individual pages for each player with:
  - **8 Personal Best Categories** - Highest score, best average, most 180s, highest checkout, best checkout rate, shortest leg, longest win streak, most legs won
  - **Performance Charts** - Track improvement over last 10 games
  - **Skill Radar** - 5-dimensional skill visualization
  - **Career Timeline** - From first to last game with all stats
  - **Achievement Showcase** - Display unlocked achievements
  - **🔥 Bed-accurate heatmap (rebuilt in v0.19.0)** - where your darts land:
    - Every real bed (single, treble, double, outer bull, bull) coloured by its share of hits — a sharp SVG at any size, following the light and dark theme
    - One warm, perceptually ordered ramp (`--m3-heat-1…6`) on a square-root scale, so one dominant bed does not grey out the rest
    - Misses shown beside the board, not on it
    - Tap a bed for hits and share; the bed list below is the keyboard and screen-reader path
    - Treble, double, bull and miss rates plus your **favourite zone** (strongest number and which neighbour your darts drift to)
    - Honest: the app records the bed, not the exact spot — so no invented scatter cloud
- **Leaderboard Rankings** - Competitive rankings in 7 categories:
  - Best Average, Most Wins, Win Rate, Most 180s, Checkout Rate, Achievements, Total Points
  - Top 3 get special medals (🏆 Gold, 🥈 Silver, 🥉 Bronze)
  - Click on any player to view their profile
- **Global Leaderboard** - Compete with players worldwide

### 👑 Admin System
- **User Management** - Complete management of all registered users
- **Subscription Control** - Grant or revoke lifetime access
- **User Statistics** - Dashboard with overall overview
- **Filter & Search** - Filter by subscription status (Trial, Active, Lifetime, Expired)
- **Delete Users** - Permanently delete users with all data
- **Real-time Updates** - Changes displayed immediately

> ⚠️ **Admin rights cannot be granted.** `is_admin` is *derived* from the email
> address (`server/src/config/adminAllowlist.ts`) and re-applied on every login —
> which grants it to the master address and **revokes** it from anyone else, even
> if a flag were set in the database. There is deliberately no `make-admin`
> endpoint.

#### Usage overview (admin only)
- **Per-user activity chart** — a 30-day bar chart of matches **and** training
  sessions. Scaled to the row's own maximum rather than a global one: the
  question is "when was this person active", not "who is most active". An idle
  day stays visible as a faint baseline tick so the row reads as a timeline.
- **Last seen** — relative ("3 days ago"), tinted by recency: green ≤ 7 days,
  neutral ≤ 30, red beyond. The exact date is in the tooltip.
- **Filter by user** on the bug-report and debug-flag tables, built from the
  entries actually present rather than from the user table.

> The data comes from `GET /api/admin/users`, which sits behind `requireAdmin`,
> so it never reaches a non-admin client.

#### Who can report what
| | Bug report | Debug flag |
|---|---|---|
| Signed-in user | ✅ create, see own | ❌ |
| Admin | ✅ see all, set status/notes | ✅ full access |

A **bug report** is user feedback and is open to every signed-in account — reachable
from the 🐞 button in the bottom-left of every screen. A **debug flag** is a
diagnostic snapshot (log buffer, screenshot, game state, route) and stays
admin-only.

## 🎯 How to Use

### 1. Create Your Profile
- On first launch, you'll see the profile selector
- Click "Neues Profil erstellen"
- Choose an avatar and enter your name
- Your profile is created and activated

### 2. Start a Game
- Click "Quick Game" from the main menu
- Select players (or create new ones on the fly)
- Choose game settings (501, legs to win, double-out)
- Click "Start Game"

### 3. Play
- **Numpad:** type the visit total and press Enter (or "Confirm") — the turn passes at once, so the next player can type straight away. A short chip shows the visit just booked.
- **Darts:** enter each dart via S/D/T and the number (exact beds, for stats and the heatmap)
- **Quick:** common scores with one tap
- **Undo:** "Take back", Backspace or Ctrl+Z returns the last visit for correction. A typed total comes back as a total ("60 → _"): type the right number, Enter — Esc keeps it. Any earlier visit of the leg in play can be corrected from its pill on the player card or the pencil in the visit history (total or single darts).
- **How was it thrown?** After a typed total the app suggests 2–3 likely decompositions (for 60, e.g. "20 · 20 · 20" or "T20 · 0 · 0"). One tap makes the darts exact; ignoring it costs nothing. Typed totals stay out of the heatmap — every heatmap says how many darts were recorded exactly.
- The checkout route sits inside the input card; every player card shows the **place in the leg** (1 green, 2 yellow, 3 orange …)
- Audio announcements guide you through the game

### 4. Score on the TV
- In a running X01 game, tap the **screen button** at the top right (tablet landscape / laptop and up)
- Drag the new window onto the TV or projector and switch to **full screen**
- The score follows every visit at once; a window opened later shows the running match straight away
- Works on the same device in the same browser (e.g. a laptop on HDMI)

### 5. Training & Tournaments
- "Training" opens six practice modes with their own statistics
- "Tournament" runs knockout and round-robin events, stored in the database

### 6. View Statistics
- Click "Statistics" from the main menu
- View your personal stats, averages, and achievements
- All data is saved and synced automatically

### 7. Switch Profiles
- Click "Profil wechseln" in the main menu or settings
- Select a different profile to view their data
- Each profile has completely separate statistics
